# Wowroom videos

Finished short videos from Wowroom (getwowroom.com), an app that will redesign a room from one photo. The app is not out yet.

The files are public so our posting tool (Metricool) can fetch each video by its link.

- One folder per video.
- The room ideas in the videos are AI pictures.
- f02: the room photo is by Martin Lang on Pexels.
- f02-v2-ledge: the room photo is an AI picture too (no stock photo). slide01-08.jpg are the TikTok photo post; the 3 mp4 files are the same video for Instagram (no song), YouTube (song) and Facebook (9:16).
- f02-v4-pillar: video 4 (a pillar in a living room). The room photo is an AI picture too. wowroom_v4_3x4.mp4 is for YouTube and Instagram, wowroom_v4_9x16.mp4 for Facebook and TikTok (same video, song included).
